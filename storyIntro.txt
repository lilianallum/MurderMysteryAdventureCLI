*** MURDER MYSTERY ***

Gwen the maid walks into Lady Rosemary's bedchamber and lays the breakfast tray on the dressing table.
She opens the curtains and the morning light floods into the room.
"Rise and shine M'lady!"
She picks the tray back up but as she turns, the tray and its contents fall to the floor with a clatter.
A blood-curdling scream echos throughout the house and George the footman followed by Mr Smith the Butler rush into the room.
But there is nothing they can do.
There is not denying it.
Lady Rosemary is dead.
